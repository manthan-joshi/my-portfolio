§ 03 / Ongoing

What I'm building this fall.

Two writing projects running September to December 2026, alongside the search for a full-time role. Both are independent, both are scoped, neither is finished. They're here so the work is visible while it's happening rather than only after.

Release Literature Review · In progress

Analytical methods for CGT release testing.

A structured review of how cell and gene therapy products get released, and why the analytics are harder than for conventional biologics. The full panel: identity, purity, potency, sterility, mycoplasma, endotoxin, residual host cell protein and DNA, replication competent virus. Vector copy number by qPCR against ddPCR and why ddPCR is taking over absolute quantitation. Flow cytometry for immunophenotyping. Why potency is the hardest section of a CGT filing. Qualification against validation, and what fit for purpose actually means in early phase. Target 4,000 to 6,000 words, fully referenced.

Drafting Sept to Nov 2026◆
Scale Comparative Analysis · In progress

Autologous against allogeneic CAR-T manufacturing.

A comparison of the two manufacturing models and why the unit economics diverge so sharply. Apheresis through to cryopreservation for each, step by step. Scale out against scale up, and why autologous cost per dose stays stubborn no matter how much volume you add. Batch failure rate, vein to vein time, manufacturing slot constraints and qualified treatment centres as a commercial bottleneck. Where allogeneic actually wins and where it doesn't. 15 to 20 pages with a cost model behind it.
